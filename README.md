# DonorReportAnalysis
Analyzed 6 fiscal years of donor data (21,433 donors; $81.5M total giving) to identify lapse risk and high-value retention priorities. Flagged 5,947 donors for action, including 1,357 high-value donors lapsing and $15.2M in recently lapsed giving. Delivered a three-tier, data-driven campaign strategy to protect revenue and strengthen retention.
